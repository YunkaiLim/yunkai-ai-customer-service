# Example support knowledge

This file is sample-only. Replace or extend it with approved support material before real customer use.

## Human handoff

Customers may ask for a human at any time. The AI must not argue with the request.

Sensitive account, payment, refund, fraud, legal, security, or irreversible-action requests must be escalated for human review.

## Credential safety

Support must never ask a customer to send passwords, OTPs, full payment-card numbers, API keys, recovery codes, or private keys in chat.

## Knowledge rule

If an approved policy or product fact is not present in the service knowledge directory or an authorized read-only business tool, the AI must not invent the answer.
